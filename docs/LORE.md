# Emberwake: the world before the rules

The sun did not die. It was locked away.

Nine hundred winters ago, the people of Veyr built a second heart for the sun so that night would never come. Their king, Ilyr, offered to carry it. The heart consumed the names of everything it illuminated. First a flower, then a village, then the king's daughter. Ilyr sealed the heart beneath the roots of the world. The light went out. The things whose names were lost became the Hollow.

Today, the cliffside city of Cinderfall survives around the Last Hearth. Its keepers burn memories for warmth. Each year the city is brighter and its people remember less. You are a keeper who finds a memory that cannot burn: a child teaching her father the word for dawn. It belongs to the Hollow King.

You steal an ember and leave the city. Three bells mark the road to the imprisoned heart. If all three ring again, the king will remember why he chose the dark. What happens after that must be a choice, not another sacrifice made on someone else's behalf.

## The road

1. **The Sunken Reach.** A drowned forest, the roofs of an old village, and moths who collect forgotten prayers. The antlered Bellwether is a warning bell given flesh. It attacks travelers because it has forgotten whom it was meant to warn.
2. **The Glass March.** An army turned to stained glass, still guarding a border erased from every map. The Oathless Sentinel remembers the command but not the cause. Its armor breaks in the rhythm of a marching song.
3. **The Rootbound Crown.** A palace grown upside down into the roots of the world. The Hollow King sits beneath an unlit sun, keeping a promise to a daughter no one else remembers.

## Keepers

- **The Warden, Asha:** a gatekeeper who remembers every person she failed to let in. Her shield is a door taken from the old city wall. Patient, resilient, fierce when cornered.
- **The Seer, Orin:** an astronomer who sees tomorrow in the ashes of yesterday. His spells are constellations the sky has forgotten. Fragile, but able to turn preparation into overwhelming force.
- **The Wayfarer, Sable:** a courier who carries letters for towns that no longer exist. She knows that venom and kindness both work slowly. Her blade steals back a little life.

## Tone and narrative contract

Quiet wonder and melancholy, with hope earned through decisions. The Hollow are victims as well as threats. Short scenes should reveal concrete lives rather than generic apocalypse exposition. Memory is the world's price, not a real-money mechanic. No energy timers, purchases, or manipulative retention systems. Replay comes from strategic routes, keeper differences, relic combinations, and discovering the complete story.

## First campaign ending

The player chooses to rekindle the sun, sharing the burden of remembrance, or keep the hearth and teach the city to preserve its stories. Both are valid victories. The king receives his daughter's name: Eira. Winning unlocks the final chronicle; later runs remain self-contained, with no permanent numerical advantage.
